### Keep Critical Windows Always Visible

Need to reference documentation while writing code? Want your calculator, translator, or other utility windows to stay on top? Quick AlwaysOnTop lets you pin or unpin any window with a single shortcut, keeping essential information visible at all times and boosting your multitasking efficiency.

### Key Features

**One-Click Pin**
Use a global hotkey or click the system tray icon to instantly pin or unpin the active window.

**Visual Indicator**
A highlighted border appears around pinned windows, making it immediately clear which windows are on top. Border appearance is fully customizable.

**Smart Notifications**
Optional sound cues and on-screen hints confirm that your action was successfully applied.

---

### Why Choose Quick AlwaysOnTop

**Faster Than Manual Methods**
No more digging through window menus to find a "stay on top" option — one hotkey is all it takes.

**Works With Any Window**
Even applications that don't natively support always-on-top behavior can be pinned with Quick AlwaysOnTop.

**Clear, Intuitive Feedback**
Visual borders and audio cues keep you informed of each window's pinned state at a glance.

---

### Use Cases

**Multitask Workflows**
Keep a reference document visible while writing or editing content in another application.

**Utility Windows**
Pin calculators, notepads, translation tools, and other small utilities to always stay in view.

**Video Conferencing**
Keep your meeting window on top while handling other tasks without missing important discussions.

---

### Getting Started

1. **Activate the Tool**
   Press the global hotkey (default: Ctrl+Shift+T)

2. **Window Pinned Automatically**
   The active window is pinned immediately, indicated by a highlighted border

3. **Unpin When Done**
   Press the hotkey again to release the window from the top position
