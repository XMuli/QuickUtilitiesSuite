### Fast inspection for real Windows workflows

Quick WindowInspector helps you identify window handles, process data, titles, bounds, DPI information, and application paths with a simpler, faster interaction model. Instead of navigating complex trees, you point at a target window and get the details you need immediately.

### Why professionals rely on it

**The essential window metadata**  
Review HWND, PID, title, rectangle, DPI, path, and other practical details in one place.

**Visual targeting that removes guesswork**  
A highlighted border shows exactly which window is being inspected, even in crowded desktop layouts.

**Made for repeated use**  
Global activation and a lightweight interface make it easy to bring it into debugging or support work all day.

---

### Where it creates leverage

**Software development**  
Capture the identifiers and metrics needed for UI debugging or automation work.

**QA and testing**  
Verify window states, sizes, and targets more quickly during manual or scripted test passes.

**IT and support**  
Inspect problem applications and identify the process behind them without extra tooling overhead.

---

### Get started in seconds

1. **Open the inspector**  
   Launch Quick WindowInspector from the tray or by shortcut.

2. **Hover the target window**  
   Move your cursor onto the window you want to inspect and watch the data update live.

3. **Use the information immediately**  
   Copy or reference the values you need for debugging, documentation, or support.
