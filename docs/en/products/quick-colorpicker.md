### Precision for design and implementation work

Quick ColorPicker gives designers, developers, and visual teams a clean way to inspect pixels, confirm color choices, and copy values in the format they need. The magnifier, fine cursor control, and multi-monitor support help you move from reference to action with less friction.

### Why it feels production-ready

**Pixel-level targeting**  
A live magnifier shows exactly what sits beneath the cursor so you can sample with confidence.

**Output that fits real workflows**  
Copy color values in practical formats such as HEX and RGB for design handoff or implementation.

**Fast activation when timing matters**  
Launch with a shortcut, adjust with the keyboard, and capture the value without changing tools.

---

### Where it fits naturally

**UI and brand design**  
Sample colors from mockups, product references, and visual systems while maintaining consistency.

**Front-end engineering**  
Check whether implemented colors match design intent without opening a separate inspection workflow.

**Creative production**  
Build palettes from screenshots, media, and live interfaces as you work.

---

### Get started in seconds

1. **Launch the picker**  
   Open Quick ColorPicker or trigger it from a global shortcut.

2. **Move to the target pixel**  
   Use the magnifier and fine controls to settle on the exact area you want.

3. **Copy the value**  
   Confirm the capture and send the chosen color format directly to the clipboard.
