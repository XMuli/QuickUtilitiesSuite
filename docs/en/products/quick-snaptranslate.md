**Turn screenshots into readable, translatable text without leaving the task in front of you.**

---

### A faster path through language barriers

Quick SnapTranslate combines capture, OCR, and translation into a single workflow that feels immediate. Whether the source is a webpage, PDF, image, subtitle, or software interface, it helps teams understand foreign-language content without the slow cycle of manual copying and separate translation tools.

### Why it is practical at work

**Capture and recognize in one motion**  
Select the content you need and let the OCR pipeline surface the text quickly.

**Translation that stays close to the source**  
Move from recognition to readable output without bouncing between multiple applications.

**Flexible privacy and connectivity options**  
Use online services when speed matters or offline OCR when local handling is preferred.

---

### Where it adds the most value

**Global teams**  
Read documentation, emails, interface copy, and support material across languages more efficiently.

**Research and education**  
Capture passages from papers, slides, or textbooks and translate them in context.

**Technical work**  
Understand foreign error messages, product screens, and manuals without slowing down troubleshooting.

---

### Get started in seconds

1. **Launch the capture action**  
   Use the shortcut or tray command to start a screenshot.

2. **Select the text region**  
   Frame the part of the screen you want to recognize and translate.

3. **Use the result immediately**  
   Copy, save, or pin the recognized and translated content where you need it.
